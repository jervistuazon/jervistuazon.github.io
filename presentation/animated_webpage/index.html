The Concept

Aurelia is conceived as an island retreat where architecture moves with the shoreline. Curved forms frame the lagoon, soften the edge between land and water, and create an experience shaped by movement, calm, and view.

Design Narrative

The resort unfolds as a sequence of connected moments: arrival, water, garden, shade, dining, and retreat.

Each curve guides the guest through the island, creating transitions that feel natural, open, and effortless.

Luxury is expressed through movement, atmosphere, and a close relationship with the landscape.

Dine with the Flow

The Restaurant

At the heart of the island, the restaurant opens toward water, palms, and sky. Designed as a refined dining pavilion, it connects indoor comfort with the atmosphere of the lagoon.

From sunrise breakfasts to intimate dinners, every moment is shaped by flavor, light, and view.
